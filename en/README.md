# Other World's — Genesis Codex

> "What if $1 could buy you a universe? Not a JPEG. Not a profile picture. A living, breathing universe with its own physics, history, biology, and civilization."

Other World's is an autonomous cosmic engine and Web3 ecosystem where procedural AI modeling meets verifiable on-chain ownership.

---

## Structure of the Codex

* **Book I — The Myth**: Cosmogony, The Void, the 81 Astrocreationauts, and the 9 Constellations.
* **Book II — The System**: Hybrid AI-Blockchain architecture, the One Impossible Law generator, and Perpetual Auction mechanics.
* **Book III — The Future**: Governance through the Pantheon DAO, algorithmic expansion, and API/SDK integrations.
* **Book IV — Art Bible**: Visual guidelines, typography, and procedural World Dossier card layouts.
* **Book V — Architect's Codex**: Technical standards, smart contract ABIs, and DNA metadata schemas.
